Focus

Freshness, confidence, mobility, and execution. The goal is to arrive at the Cincinnati Spartan Trifecta Weekend healthy, energized, and spring‑loaded, not fatigued.

Goal

Arrive at race weekend healthy, energized, and spring‑loaded.

Mindset: you already did the work. This is the "don’t mess it up" month.

Freshness Mobility Execution

Taper rules

  • ✓Reduce volume 40–60%
  • ✓Keep intensity, cut total reps
  • ✓Prioritize sleep and hydration
  • ✓No new movements

Race week structure

Early week: light strength + short runs. Midweek: mobility and strides. 48 hours out: rest, gear and fueling check. Race day: calm execution.

Monday: Light Strength + Grip Tune‑Up

  • ✓Pull-ups: 3 × easy reps (leave 3–4 in the tank)
  • ✓Dead hang: 2 × 20–30 sec
  • ✓Light squats: 2 × 8 (bodyweight or very light)
  • ✓Light bench or dumbbell press: 2 × 8
  • ✓Core (1–2 rounds): dead bug 10/10 • side plank 20 sec each

Purpose: maintain muscle activation without fatigue.

Tuesday: Easy Run

  • ✓2–3 miles at a very easy pace
  • ✓5–8 minutes hip & hamstring mobility

Purpose: keep legs loose and aerobic system awake.

Wednesday: Movement Only

Choose one. No strength. No intensity.

  • ✓30–40 minute walk
  • ✓Gentle bike
  • ✓Light trails
  • ✓Stretch + mobility

Thursday: Optional Easy Shakeout

If you feel good:

  • ✓1–2 mile jog OR 20–30 minute walk
  • ✓Light mobility

If you feel tired: skip entirely.

Purpose: keep your engine awake without adding stress.

Friday: Travel / Rest / Final Prep

  • ✓Walk only
  • ✓Stretch lightly
  • ✓Hydrate well
  • ✓Increase carbs slightly
  • ✓Pack gear and mentally review race weekend

Goal: enter race weekend well rested.

48 hours out: simple checklist

  • ✓Sleep opportunity: earlier bedtime
  • ✓Hydration: steady all day
  • ✓Mobility: short, easy, daily
  • ✓Nothing spicy: no hero workouts

Race weekend

Calm execution wins. Smooth pacing, steady fueling, smart grip management, and efficient transitions.

Saturday: Beast (June 6)

Start: 10:00 AM (OPEN)

Race-day strategy

  • ✓Start slow
  • ✓Power hike any steep hills
  • ✓Save grip for obstacles (shake arms often)
  • ✓Stay ahead on fluids and electrolytes
  • ✓Keep heart rate under control for first 60–70%

Post race

  • ✓Eat immediately (carbs + protein)
  • ✓Light stretching
  • ✓Hydrate aggressively

Sunday AM: Super (June 7)

Start: 9:30 AM

Strategy

  • ✓Legs will be tired: keep pace conservative
  • ✓Run flats, hike hills
  • ✓Smooth and steady on obstacles
  • ✓Don’t chase speed

Post race

  • ✓Eat + hydrate
  • ✓Light mobility

Sunday PM: Sprint (June 7)

Start: 2:00 PM

Strategy

  • ✓This is survival + fun
  • ✓Pace easy
  • ✓Focus on clean obstacle execution
  • ✓Use whatever strength remains to finish strong

Finish goal: complete the full Trifecta Weekend feeling proud, steady, and healthy.

Simple weekend mantra

Smooth is fast. Calm is strong. Finish proud.
