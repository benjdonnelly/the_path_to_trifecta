January 2026 Base Strength & Technique

January: Build the base

Build strength, restore mobility, and restart the running engine without overtraining.

Weekly schedule Progression targets Summary & highlights

Theme

Build strength, restore mobility, and restart the running engine without overtraining. This month is about clean reps, consistent movement, and protecting recovery.

What matters most

  • ✓Technique first: leave 2 reps in the tank most sets.
  • ✓Controlled running: easy, conversational effort.
  • ✓Daily mobility: hips, hamstrings, piriformis.
  • ✓Grip foundation: hangs show up weekly.

Goal

finish strong, not tired.

Rule: Mobility is the daily tax that keeps training on track.

Weekly schedule

Repeat this structure weekly. Adjust load and volume using the progression targets below.

Monday: Strength (Upper Body)

  • ✓Pull-ups: 3 × max reps (band if needed)
  • ✓Hanging practice: 3 × 20–30 sec
  • ✓Push-ups: 3 × 10–15
  • ✓Dumbbell press or bench press: 3 × 8–10
  • ✓Rows: 3 × 10
  • ✓Core: 2 × 30 sec plank

Tuesday: Easy Run + Mobility

  • ✓Easy run: 2–3 miles (Zone 2)
  • ✓Hip + hamstring mobility: 10 minutes
  • ✓Piriformis stretch routine

Wednesday: Strength (Lower Body + Core)

  • ✓Squats (light to moderate): 3 × 10
  • ✓Lunges: 3 × 12 each leg
  • ✓Deadlift (light): 3 × 8
  • ✓Step-ups: 3 × 10
  • ✓Farmer carry: 3 × 30–45 sec
  • ✓Core circuit (3 rounds): plank / dead bug / bird dog

Thursday: Grip + Hanging Day

  • ✓Dead hangs: 3 × 30–45 sec
  • ✓Towel hangs or rope grip: 2 × 20 sec
  • ✓Monkey bar practice (if available)
  • ✓Finisher: 10 slow, smooth burpees

Friday: Optional Recovery

Optional recovery day. May be skipped if needed.

  • ✓Walk: 30–45 minutes
  • ✓Mobility: 10 minutes
  • ✓Light stretching

Saturday: Long Run Progression

All runs slow, steady, and conversational.

  • ✓Week 1: 3 miles
  • ✓Week 2: 4 miles
  • ✓Week 3: 4 miles
  • ✓Week 4: 5 miles

Sunday: Rest

  • ✓Rest / church / family
  • ✓Light dog walks only
  • ✓Gentle stretching as needed
  • ✓No structured workout

Progression targets

Small changes only. The goal is momentum and clean form, not maxing out.

Week by week

  • ✓Week 1: establish working weights and clean form (leave 2 reps in the tank).
  • ✓Week 2: add 1 rep per set or +2.5 to 5 lb to key lifts.
  • ✓Week 3: keep load, tighten rest times, add one extra quality set to pulling.
  • ✓Week 4: deload (reduce volume by about 30–40%), keep movement patterns sharp.

Safety checks

  • ✓If pain spikes, downshift volume and prioritize mobility.
  • ✓If sleep drops hard for 2 nights, keep intensity low and simplify the session.
  • ✓Most sets stop 1–2 reps before failure.

The final stretch of the month added a quality interval run plus a solid 6-mile steady session (100 minutes total with warmup/cooldown), and I practiced the exact skill that matters on course: staying in control by downshifting when HR drifted, guided by breathing first. Biggest win: the system held steady, and I'm already spotting the next lever, fueling after ~4 miles to keep legs from going heavy late-run..
