Strength-first base build (January to March 2026):

Build upper-body strength and total-body power while keeping moderate, repeatable endurance work. This block builds your engine without stealing recovery from strength gains.

Rules of the road

  • ✓Strength is the main event: 3 focused lifts per week, plus 1 short grip session.
  • ✓Cardio is controlled: 2 runs per week (one easy, one mixed), plus optional Zone 2 cross-training.
  • ✓Mobility is non-negotiable: 10 minutes after every session, plus 1 longer reset day weekly.
  • ✓Progress slowly: add small reps or small load weekly, stop 1 to 2 reps before failure most sets.
Upper body strength Grip durability Moderate endurance Recovery first

Purpose

Build a repeatable training groove that stacks strength gains while keeping your aerobic base alive.

North Star

Finish strong, not tired.

Build the base: habits, strength, grip, and controlled running mileage.

January: Foundation Month

Theme: Restart → Rebuild → Reset

  • ✓Light strength to build habits
  • ✓Easy runs 2 to 5 miles
  • ✓Begin hanging work
  • ✓Restore mobility (hips, hamstrings, piriformis)
  • ✓Protect sleep and adjust schedule rhythm
  • ✓No heavy loads yet

February: Strength & Grip Month

Theme: Lift + Hang + Build

  • ✓Strength increases weekly
  • ✓Hanging endurance grows (30 to 60 sec holds)
  • ✓Runs extend to 5 to 7 miles
  • ✓Introduce moderate deadlifts
  • ✓Carry strength begins
Open February

Theme: Add power + hills + obstacle practice

  • ✓Hill workouts start (treadmill incline or trails)
  • ✓Power movements: step-ups, box work, sled substitutions
  • ✓Heavier lifting (safe ramp from February)
  • ✓Long runs 7 to 9 miles
  • ✓Begin mixed workouts: run + hang, run + burpees, run + carries
Open March

Strength-to-Performance (April to June 2026)

Convert strength into race-ready performance. April and May build volume and specificity. June tapers volume so you arrive fresh, strong, and confident.

Rules of the road

  • ✓Strength stays on the schedule: keep 2–3 lifts per week so power doesn’t leak.
  • ✓Hybrid specificity: pair grip + carries + movement so obstacles feel normal under fatigue.
  • ✓Run volume builds, then tapers: longer weekends in April/May, no long runs in early June.
  • ✓Pacing + fueling are trained: practice what you’ll do on race day (not just fitness).
  • ✓Recovery is a skill: mobility after sessions, one reset day weekly, sleep consistency guarded.
  • ✓Downshift fast: if pain or sleep/fatigue spikes, reduce volume 20–30% and prioritize mobility/sleep.
Obstacle durability Hybrid endurance Pacing + fueling Arrive fresh

Purpose

Turn your strength base into race-ready performance: durable grip, confident carries, and steady movement when tired.

North Star

Arrive rested, confident, spring-loaded.

Early June: Taper focus

  • ✓Light strength, light running, lots of mobility
  • ✓No long runs
  • ✓Technical practice: hanging, rope climb, carries
  • ✓Sleep and nutrition dialed in
  • ✓Goal: Walk into June 6th feeling spring-loaded

April simulates, May peaks, June sharpens and executes the weekend.

April: Simulation Month

Theme: Put it all together

  • ✓Simulated obstacle circuits
  • ✓Longer weekend runs (8 to 10 miles)
  • ✓Grip + carry supersets
  • ✓Introduce one double session weekly (AM strength + PM jog, or vice versa)
  • ✓Mobility and recovery emphasized
  • ✓Goal: Move well under fatigue
Open April

May: Peak Month

Theme: High volume, full Spartan-style training

  • ✓Peak grip strength
  • ✓Peak long run 11 to 13 miles
  • ✓Back to back training days (Sat + Sun)
  • ✓Race-pace efforts on short runs
  • ✓Full-body strength at highest load
  • ✓Mock Sprint/Super hybrid workouts
  • ✓Goal: Arrive durable, confident, and ready
Open May

June: Race Month

Final sharpening, then execute the weekend.

  • ✓Reduce volume, keep intensity controlled
  • ✓Practice obstacles fresh, not fried
  • ✓Dial pacing, fueling, and transitions
  • ✓Prioritize sleep consistency
Open June
