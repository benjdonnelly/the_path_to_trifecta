March 2026 Power + Endurance Blend

March: Convert strength into Spartan power

Build Beast legs, enhance grip strength, improve obstacle confidence, and increase running durability.

Race focus

  • ✓ 🍀 Irish Double · March 15
    8K + 5K run
Weekly schedule Progression targets Summary & highlights

Theme

Convert strength into Spartan-specific power and toughness. This month adds hills and obstacle conditioning while keeping recovery guarded.

What matters most

  • ✓: moderate strength + step-ups + long runs stay steady.
  • ✓Grip confidence: hangs show up Monday and in Thursday circuits.
  • ✓Terrain toughness: hills on Tuesday build climbing strength safely.
  • ✓Obstacle conditioning: Thursday blends hang, burpees, carries, and jogging.

Goal

Build Beast legs, enhance grip strength, improve obstacle confidence, and increase running durability.

Rule: stay smooth. Hard effort comes from consistency, not chaos.

Weekly schedule

Repeat this structure weekly. Hills and obstacle circuits add spice, but recovery stays protected.

Monday: Strength (Upper Body Power + Hang)

  • ✓Pull-ups: 4 × max reps (band if needed)
  • ✓Negative pull-ups: 3 × 3 slow (5–7 sec descents)
  • ✓Dead hang: 3 × 40–60 sec
  • ✓Dumbbell or barbell bench: 4 × 6
  • ✓Bent-over rows: 4 × 8–10
  • ✓Push-ups: 3 × 15
  • ✓Core: hollow hold 3 × 20 sec

Tuesday: Hill Run + Mobility

Choose one:

  • ✓Treadmill incline: 20–25 min at 6–10% incline (easy pace)
  • ✓Trail hills: 30 min run/walk mixed climbing

Then:

  • ✓Hip mobility: 8–10 minutes
  • ✓Piriformis activation: 2–3 minutes

Wednesday: Strength (Lower Body Power + Carry Prep)

  • ✓Squats: 4 × 6 (moderate weight)
  • ✓Deadlift: 3 × 5 (moderate weight)
  • ✓Step-ups (tall box): 3 × 12
  • ✓Lunges: 3 × 10 each leg
  • ✓Farmer carry: 4 × 60 sec
  • ✓Core circuit: plank 45 sec • dead bug 10/10 • side plank 20 sec each side

Thursday: Spartan Obstacle Conditioning

Circuit: 3 rounds (rest 1–2 min between rounds)

  • ✓30 sec dead hang
  • ✓10 burpees (steady pace)
  • ✓10 push-ups
  • ✓20 air squats
  • ✓30 sec farmer carry
  • ✓200m jog

Then: optional monkey bar practice 5–10 minutes

Friday: Active Recovery

Choose one. No intensity, focus on recovery.

  • ✓45-minute walk
  • ✓Light bike
  • ✓Easy elliptical
  • ✓Stretch + mobility

Saturday: Long Run Progression

Runs remain slow, comfortable, conversational.

  • ✓Week 9: 7 miles
  • ✓Week 10: 8 miles
  • ✓Week 11: 8 miles
  • ✓Week 12: 9 miles

Sunday: Rest / Family Day

  • ✓Light dog walks only
  • ✓Gentle stretching if needed
  • ✓No structured workout

Progression targets

Small changes only. The goal is momentum and clean form, not maxing out.

Week by week

  • ✓Week 1: establish working weights and clean form (leave 2 reps in the tank).
  • ✓Week 2: add 1 rep per set or +2.5 to 5 lb to key lifts.
  • ✓Week 3: keep load, tighten rest times, add one extra quality set to pulling.
  • ✓Week 4: deload (reduce volume by about 30–40%), keep movement patterns sharp.

Safety checks

  • ✓If pain spikes, downshift volume and prioritize mobility.
  • ✓If sleep drops hard for 2 nights, keep intensity low and simplify the session.
  • ✓Most sets stop 1–2 reps before failure.
