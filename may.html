May 2026 Peak Race Build

May: Peak durability, peak confidence

Race simulations, compromised running, and endurance peak. Build maximum durability before tapering in early June.

Race focus

  • ✓ 🏔️ Trifecta Weekend · June 6–7
    Beast 21K • Super 10K • Sprint 5K
Weekly schedule Progression targets Summary & highlights

Theme

Race simulations, compromised running, endurance peak. May is high intent: you build durability, confidence, and fitness, then protect recovery so you can actually absorb it.

What matters most

  • ✓Simulation quality: Thursday feels like race day (smooth + controlled).
  • ✓Carry stamina: longer carries show up Wednesday and Thursday.
  • ✓Long-run control: you go long, but never sloppy.
  • ✓Recovery protected: Friday is non-negotiable.

Goal

Build maximum durability, confidence, and fitness before tapering in early June.

Rule: peak training only works if sleep, fueling, and recovery stay locked.

Weekly schedule

Repeat this structure weekly. Keep transitions smooth and stop most sets 1–2 reps before failure.

Monday: Peak Strength (Full Body + Hang)

  • ✓Pull-ups: 5 × max reps
  • ✓Negative pull-ups: 3 × 3 (slow descents)
  • ✓Dead hang: 3 × 60–75 sec
  • ✓Barbell squat or dumbbell squat: 4 × 5 (moderate to heavy)
  • ✓Bench press or dumbbell press: 4 × 5–6
  • ✓Bent-over rows: 4 × 8
  • ✓Core (3 rounds): 25 sec hollow hold • 10/10 dead bugs • 30 sec side planks

Purpose: peak strength for carries, rigs, rope climb, and obstacles.

Tuesday: Mid-Week Endurance Run

  • ✓4–5 miles easy (Zone 2)

Then:

  • ✓Mobility: 10 minutes (hips, hamstrings, calves)
  • ✓Piriformis routine: 2–3 minutes

Purpose: maintain aerobic base without draining recovery.

Wednesday: Power + Carries

  • ✓Step-ups (tall box): 4 × 15 each leg
  • ✓Lunges: 3 × 12 each leg
  • ✓Deadlift: 3 × 5 (moderate)
  • ✓Farmer carry: 4 × 75–90 sec
  • ✓Sandbag substitute: heavy dumbbell hold or double-kettlebell front carry 3 × 30–45 sec
  • ✓Core: plank × 60 sec

Purpose: build Beast leg strength and carry stamina.

Thursday: Spartan Simulation Day

Complete 3 rounds (2–3 min rest between):

  • ✓45 sec dead hang
  • ✓400–600m run
  • ✓15 burpees
  • ✓15 push-ups
  • ✓25 air squats
  • ✓60 sec farmer carry
  • ✓200m run (optional finisher)

Then: 5–10 minutes monkey bar or hanging practice

Purpose: true race-style conditioning: strength + grip + running.

Friday: Active Recovery

This day is non-negotiable to protect recovery.

  • ✓45–60 min walk
  • ✓Easy bike or elliptical
  • ✓Long mobility session
  • ✓Gentle stretching

Saturday: Peak Long Run Progression

All runs slow, conversational, and controlled.

  • ✓Week 17: 10 miles
  • ✓Week 18: 11 miles
  • ✓Week 19: 12 miles
  • ✓Week 20: 10–12 miles (choose based on energy)

Purpose: build Beast-ready endurance without breaking down.

Sunday: Rest / Family Day

  • ✓Light dog walks only
  • ✓Gentle stretching if needed
  • ✓No structured training

Progression targets

Use these as guardrails so you peak without breaking down.

Week by week

  • ✓Week 1: establish working weights and clean form (leave 2 reps in the tank).
  • ✓Week 2: add 1 rep per set or +2.5 to 5 lb to key lifts.
  • ✓Week 3: keep load, tighten rest times, add one extra quality set to pulling.
  • ✓Week 4: deload (reduce volume by about 30–40%), keep movement patterns sharp.

Safety checks

  • ✓If pain spikes, downshift volume and prioritize mobility.
  • ✓If sleep drops hard for 2 nights, keep intensity low and simplify the session.
  • ✓Most sets stop 1–2 reps before failure.
