April 2026 Race Build Phase I

April: Simulate, resist fatigue, move well

Spartan simulation, fatigue resistance, and unified strength + endurance. Goal: move well under fatigue and handle long durations.

Race focus

  • ✓ 🏃‍♂️‍➡️ Owens Corning · April 26
    Half marathon
Weekly schedule Progression targets Summary & highlights

Theme

Spartan Simulation • Fatigue Resistance • Unified Strength + Endurance. This month blends obstacles with running so movement under fatigue becomes normal.

What matters most

  • ✓Unified strength: full-body work that supports obstacle flow.
  • ✓Run durability: moderate run + hills and longer weekends without strain.
  • ✓Simulation: Thursday circuits blend grip, strength, and running.
  • ✓Recovery protected: Friday is repair, not fitness.

Goal

Move well under fatigue, handle long durations, and blend obstacles with running.

Rule: train like you want to race: smooth effort, clean transitions, no hero-pacing.

Simulation Fatigue resistance Unified strength

Weekly schedule

Repeat this structure weekly. Keep the intent of each day (purpose) and adjust volume using the progression targets below.

Monday: Full-Body Strength + Hang Endurance

  • ✓Pull-ups: 4 × max reps
  • ✓Assisted pull-ups or negatives: 3 × 4–6
  • ✓Dead hang: 3 × 45–60 sec
  • ✓Barbell or dumbbell squat: 4 × 6–8
  • ✓Bench press or dumbbell press: 4 × 6–8
  • ✓Rows: 3 × 10
  • ✓Core circuit (3 rounds): hollow hold 20–25 sec • dead bug 10/10 • side plank 25 sec each

Purpose: build unified upper + lower body strength for obstacle flow.

Tuesday: Moderate Run + Hill Work

  • ✓2 miles easy
  • ✓Then 10–12 minutes hill repeats: 30–45 sec uphill jog • walk down recovery

Finish with:

  • ✓Hip + hamstring mobility: 10 minutes
  • ✓Piriformis work: 2–3 minutes

Purpose: build climbing legs and durability.

Wednesday: Strength (Carries + Power)

  • ✓Step-ups (tall box): 3 × 15 each leg
  • ✓Lunges: 3 × 12 each leg
  • ✓Deadlift: 3 × 5 (moderate)
  • ✓Farmer carry: 4 × 60–75 sec
  • ✓Overhead kettlebell or dumbbell hold: 3 × 20–30 sec
  • ✓Core finisher: plank × 60 sec

Purpose: carry control + leg strength for Beast terrain.

Thursday: Spartan Simulation Day

Repeat 3 rounds (rest 2 min between):

  • ✓Dead hang: 30 sec
  • ✓400m run
  • ✓12 burpees
  • ✓15 push-ups
  • ✓20 air squats
  • ✓45 sec farmer carry

Then optionally: monkey bars / rig practice 5–10 min

Purpose: blend strength, grip, and running under controlled fatigue.

Friday: Active Recovery

Choose one. No intensity. Your body repairs here.

  • ✓45–60 minute walk
  • ✓Gentle bike or elliptical
  • ✓Long mobility session (hips, hamstrings, back)
  • ✓Recovery stretching

Saturday: Long Run Progression

Build Beast endurance without strain. Pace remains slow and conversational.

  • ✓Week 13: 8 miles
  • ✓Week 14: 9 miles
  • ✓Week 15: 10 miles
  • ✓Week 16: 10 miles

Sunday: Full Rest / Light Walks

  • ✓Dog walks only
  • ✓Gentle stretching if needed
  • ✓No structured sessions

Progression targets

Progress one variable at a time. April is about durability and practice, not ego-lifting.

Week by week

  • ✓Week 1: keep loads steady and learn the Thursday simulation rhythm (smooth transitions).
  • ✓Week 2: add a small run volume bump (or +2.5–5 lb on 1–2 key lifts).
  • ✓Week 3: tighten rest times and improve movement quality under fatigue.
  • ✓Week 4: deload 30–40% volume if needed, stay sharp, protect race freshness.

Safety checks

  • ✓If pain spikes, reduce volume 20–30% and prioritize mobility.
  • ✓If sleep drops hard for 2 nights, keep intensity low and simplify the session.
  • ✓Most sets stop 1–2 reps before failure.
