February 2026 Strength Upgrade & Work Capacity

February: Strength up, grip up, endurance up

Prepare the body for true Spartan demands without overtraining. Progress slowly and safely.

Weekly schedule Progression targets Summary & highlights

Theme

Strength up • Grip up • Endurance up (slowly and safely). Build work capacity without stealing recovery.

What matters most

  • ✓Upper body power ramps with more pulling and pressing volume.
  • ✓Grip durability grows via longer hangs and rope simulation.
  • ✓Endurance stays controlled: long run builds gradually, fully conversational.
  • ✓Recovery protects progress: active recovery choices on Friday.

Goal

Prepare the body for true Spartan demands without overtraining.

Rule: progress slowly, stop 1–2 reps before failure most sets.

Weekly schedule

Repeat this structure weekly. Adjust load and volume using the progression targets below.

Monday: Strength (Upper Body Power)

  • ✓Pull-ups: 4 × max reps (band if needed)
  • ✓Negative pull-ups: 3 × 3 slow descents
  • ✓Hang practice: 3 × 30–40 sec
  • ✓Dumbbell or barbell bench press: 4 × 6–8
  • ✓Dumbbell rows: 4 × 8–10
  • ✓Push-ups: 3 × 12–15
  • ✓Core: side planks 3 × 30 sec each side

Tuesday: Easy Run + Mobility

  • ✓Easy run: 3–4 miles (comfortable Zone 2)
  • ✓Hip + hamstring mobility: 10 minutes
  • ✓Piriformis activation + stretch: 3–5 minutes

Wednesday: Strength (Lower Body + Carry Power)

  • ✓Squats: 4 × 8
  • ✓Lunges or Bulgarian split squats: 3 × 10 each leg
  • ✓Deadlifts (moderate): 3 × 6
  • ✓Step-ups (tall box): 3 × 10
  • ✓Farmer carry: 4 × 45–60 sec
  • ✓Core circuit (3 rounds): dead bug 10/10 • bird dog 10/10 • hollow hold 20 sec

Thursday: Grip Day (Spartan Obstacle Prep)

  • ✓Dead hangs: 4 × 40–60 sec
  • ✓Towel hangs (rope simulation): 3 × 20–30 sec
  • ✓Ring rows or TRX rows: 3 × 12
  • ✓Kettlebell holds: 3 × 30 sec
  • ✓Monkey bar practice (if available)
  • ✓Finisher: 12 slow, controlled burpees

Friday: Active Recovery

Choose one:

  • ✓45-minute walk
  • ✓Light bike session
  • ✓Full-body stretching and mobility
  • ✓Easy elliptical

Saturday: Long Run Progression

All long runs slow, comfortable, fully conversational.

  • ✓Week 5: 5 miles
  • ✓Week 6: 6 miles
  • ✓Week 7: 6 miles
  • ✓Week 8: 7 miles

Sunday: Rest / Family Day

  • ✓Light dog walks only
  • ✓Gentle stretching if needed
  • ✓No structured workout

Progression targets

Small changes only. The goal is momentum and clean form, not maxing out.

Week by week

  • ✓Week 1: establish working weights and clean form (leave 2 reps in the tank).
  • ✓Week 2: add 1 rep per set or +2.5 to 5 lb to key lifts.
  • ✓Week 3: keep load, tighten rest times, add one extra quality set to pulling.
  • ✓Week 4: deload (reduce volume by about 30–40%), keep movement patterns sharp.

Safety checks

  • ✓If pain spikes, downshift volume and prioritize mobility.
  • ✓If sleep drops hard for 2 nights, keep intensity low and simplify the session.
  • ✓Most sets stop 1–2 reps before failure.
